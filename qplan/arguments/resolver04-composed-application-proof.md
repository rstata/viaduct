It instantiates the finite structural evaluator from `tla/ProviderPathEvaluation.tla` at that root cell and requires the provider read to equal the resulting validated-path value. `tla/ProviderReadsProof.tla` proves that the provider-root prefix cell and final cell are the same immutable exact-key cell, hence prefix and final structural provider reads agree and constructed bindings conform to final reads. `tla/Resolver04ApplicationProof.tla` separately derives that each binding's variable site precedes its defining field in the unified site order.

`tla/Resolver04Projection.tla` unions Resolver03 supplied demand with exact ambient operation, provider, and sibling contributions. Its proof derives projection coverage for direct, guarded nested, and ambient requirements. The final application proof transfers Resolver03's five non-variable `correctResolution` conjuncts to the Resolver04 tree, proves variable-name and binding-value conformance from final provider reads, and combines those facts with occurrence-product termination as `Resolver04ApplicationsAreCorrect`.

The proof still assumes that Kotlin provider selections and values induce the finite path trace and ranked list-conversion equations, and that variable substitution and ambient-contribution extraction are faithful. It also assumes the finite result, work-item, and observation atoms correctly represent Kotlin objects, lists, materialization, union, and resolver comparison.
