# Argument for [resolver04-variable-order-and-ambient-seal-proof]

`tla/Resolver04.tla` adds field and variable sites to one finite dependency-first `SiteOrder`. TLAPS proves that every provider dependency has a strictly earlier sequence position, every variable referenced by a required field fragment is among those earlier dependencies, resolved bindings equal their stipulated provider reads, and every exact-field ambient contribution is included in the field's aggregated demand. These lemmas combine with Resolver03's guarded producer-completeness theorem in `Resolver04LocalCorrectness`.

The ambient-seal result assumes contribution discovery is complete before the target field is constructed; this is the formal non-lateness obligation corresponding to Resolver04's ambient-selection retargeting. `tla/Resolver04ApplicationProof.tla` now composes the ordering and ambient-demand results with stable provider reads and every extensional result-tree conjunct as recorded by [resolver04-composed-application-proof]. Provider-path validity, substitution semantics, precise input compatibility, and Kotlin-to-TLA refinement remain explicit assumptions. `Resolver04MC` checks the local site model, while `Resolver04ApplicationMC` checks a composed model with a nonempty binding and eventual `CorrectResolution`.
